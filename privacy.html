Privacy

This page explains how the National Incubation Center Sialkot reporting system handles information. It applies to the startups in NIC Sialkot's incubation programme and to the NIC Sialkot team.

What the system holds

  • What startups send in their quarterly reports: answers about their business, figures, and the files they upload.
  • The team's records of the programme: sessions, attendance, feedback, and the work on each report.

Where it is kept

Everything is kept in NIC Sialkot's own Google account, in Google Sheets and Google Drive. The system's server works only with NIC Sialkot's reporting sheet, the sheets and folders the reporting draws on, and the session feedback form. It has no other use of the Google account.

What it is used for

Only to run the incubation programme and to prepare the reports NIC Sialkot owes under its programme agreement. The information is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone outside that purpose.

Who can see it

A startup sees its own report through its own private link. The NIC Sialkot team sees what it needs to run the programme and to prepare the reports.

Questions

Startups and team members can ask the NIC Sialkot team about their information, or ask for it to be corrected, through the programme's usual contact.
